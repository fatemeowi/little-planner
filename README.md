# ✦ Little Planner

*A little space to organize your days, one small step at a time.*

Little Planner is a cozy, personal dashboard for keeping study tasks, daily habits, and weekly plans in one place. It’s made to feel calm and colorful, without turning your life into a spreadsheet with commitment issues.

## What’s inside?

- **Assignment planner** — organize coursework, deadlines, priorities, and progress.
- **Habit tracker** — keep track of daily habits and build consistency over time.
- **Weekly planner** — lay out your tasks for the week and mark them as complete.
- **Persian & English** — switch the interface language and text direction.
- **Jalali dates in Persian mode** — dates are displayed using the Solar Hijri calendar when Persian is selected; English mode uses Gregorian dates.
- **Light & dark themes** — choose the mood that works for you.
- **Quick cleanup** — clear the selected day’s planner data without wiping everything.
- **Local saving** — your planner data and preferences are saved in your browser with `localStorage`.

## Get started

No installation, packages, or build tools needed. A rare moment of mercy from technology.

1. Download and extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the `little_planner_site` folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.

For a smoother development workflow, open the folder in **Visual Studio Code** and run `index.html` with the **Live Server** extension.

No frameworks required. Just the web basics doing their job.

## A tiny note about your data

Little Planner currently stores information in the browser you use. That means your data won’t automatically sync across devices or browsers. Clearing browser storage may also erase it, so export or back up anything important before clearing site data.

There is no sign-in or sign-up flow in this version. Open the planner and get straight to planning.

## Before publishing online

This is a front-end project. If you publish it as a static website, each visitor’s data remains local to their own browser. Cross-device syncing, shared accounts, and secure user accounts would require a backend or a trusted authentication service.
